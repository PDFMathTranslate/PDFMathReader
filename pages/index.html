On the Convergence of Stochastic Gradient Descent

We study the convergence of stochastic gradient descent under nonconvex objectives and derive a new upper bound on the rate.
E‖∇f(xt)‖² ≤ O(1/√T)
Here T denotes the number of iterations. Formulas, symbols and layout are preserved after translation.

2. Preliminaries

Let f be an L-smooth function. Then for all x, y the gradient satisfies the Lipschitz condition.
‖∇f(x) − ∇f(y)‖ ≤ L‖x − y‖
We assume that the variance of the stochastic gradient is bounded.

3. Main Result

Theorem 1: Under the assumptions above, with step size η = 1/√T the algorithm converges at the stated rate.
